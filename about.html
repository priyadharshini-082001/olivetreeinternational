WELCOME TO OUR SCHOOL

Little Beginnings, Bright Futures!

Established in 2018, Olive Tree International Kindergarten School, Chennai, is dedicated to nurturing young minds through joyful learning, meaningful experiences and compassionate care. We believe that every child is unique, curious and full of wonderful possibilities. Our kindergarten approach encourages children to discover the world through play, exploration, creativity and positive social interaction. At Olive Tree, education goes beyond letters and numbers. We focus on building confidence, communication skills, good values, independence and a lifelong love for learning in a warm, welcoming environment.
