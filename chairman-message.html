Chairman's Message

A vision to inspire young minds, nurture strong values and shape a brighter tomorrow.

OUR CHAIRMAN

Dr. P. Isaac Livingstone

Olive Tree Group of Schools

FROM THE CHAIRMAN'S DESK
“

Dear Parents, Students and Well-Wishers,

Education has the power to shape not only the future of a child but also the future of society. At Olive Tree, our responsibility is therefore much greater than simply imparting academic knowledge. We endeavour to create a dynamic learning environment where children develop the ability to think, question, explore and apply what they learn. Our focus is to provide quality education in an atmosphere that nurtures curiosity, confidence, discipline and strong values. The world our children will inherit is increasingly interconnected and competitive. They must therefore be equipped with academic competence as well as communication skills, creativity, adaptability, leadership qualities and a global outlook. At the same time, true education must develop character. We want our students to grow into individuals who demonstrate integrity, compassion, responsibility and respect for others. Parents are indispensable partners in this educational journey. I sincerely thank every parent who has placed their confidence in Olive Tree. Together, we will continue to provide our children with an education that prepares them to lead meaningful and successful lives.

Warm regards,

Dr. P. Isaac Livingstone

Chairman · Olive Tree Group of Schools
