School Discipline

Attendance & Punctuality

Regular attendance and punctuality are essential for academic progress. Students should arrive at school on time, attend classes consistently, and participate actively in daily learning activities. These habits develop responsibility and effective time management.

Uniform & Personal Grooming

Students are expected to wear the prescribed school uniform neatly and maintain personal cleanliness. Proper grooming encourages self-respect, confidence, equality, and a strong sense of belonging. Every child should maintain a presentable appearance.

Classroom Discipline

Students should listen attentively, follow teachers' guidance, complete assigned work, and maintain a peaceful learning environment. Respectful classroom behaviour supports meaningful learning, active participation, and academic excellence.

Respect & Good Behaviour

Every student should treat teachers, classmates, staff, and visitors with kindness and respect. Polite communication, honesty, cooperation, and empathy help create a welcoming and supportive school community where everyone feels valued.

Safety & Responsibility

Students must follow safety instructions, respect school property, and behave responsibly throughout the campus. Safe conduct during classroom sessions, playtime, and school activities protects everyone's wellbeing and creates a secure environment.

Cleanliness & Environment

Students are encouraged to keep classrooms, playgrounds, and shared spaces clean. Caring for nature, reducing waste, and protecting school resources develop environmental awareness and responsible everyday habits for a sustainable future.
