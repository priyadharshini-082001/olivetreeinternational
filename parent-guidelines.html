A PARTNERSHIP FOR EVERY CHILD

Growing Together.
Guiding Every Child.

A child's journey becomes more meaningful when home and school work together. These guidelines help us build a positive, safe and inspiring learning environment.

Olive Tree Global School
01
DAILY ROUTINE

Attendance & Punctuality

Ensure regular attendance and timely arrival. Inform the school about absences and follow the prescribed leave procedure.

02
STAY CONNECTED

School Communication

Read school circulars and announcements regularly. Communicate with teachers through approved channels whenever support or clarification is needed.

03
LEARNING HABITS

Learning at Home

Encourage reading, curiosity and independent work. Provide a calm study space and help your child establish a consistent learning routine.

04
HEALTH & HAPPINESS

Health & Well-being

Support healthy meals, sufficient sleep and hydration. Inform the school of relevant health concerns and keep your child home when unwell.

05
READY FOR SCHOOL

Uniform & Preparation

Follow the school's uniform and grooming guidelines. Check that your child brings the required books, stationery and learning materials each day.

06
VALUES & CHARACTER

Respect & Responsibility

Nurture kindness, honesty and respect for others. Encourage your child to take responsibility and resolve disagreements peacefully.

07
ACTIVE PARTICIPATION

Meetings & Activities

Attend parent-teacher meetings and take an interest in school activities. Work with teachers to support your child's progress and development.

08
A SAFE ENVIRONMENT

Student Safety

Follow school arrival, dispersal and visitor procedures. Ensure authorised persons collect your child and report changes promptly.

OUR SHARED COMMITMENT

Every child deserves the opportunity to flourish.

Thank you for partnering with us to nurture confident, compassionate and curious learners.

About Us

Olive Tree International School nurtures young minds through joyful learning, creative activities and foundational education. We encourage curiosity, confidence, good values and all-round development in every child.
