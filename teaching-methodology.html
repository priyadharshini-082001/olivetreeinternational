HOW WE TEACH

Learning That Inspires Every Child

At Olive Tree Global School, we believe that every child learns differently. Our teaching methodology combines joyful experiences, practical exploration, creativity and meaningful interaction to build confident, curious and independent learners.

✦ Child-Centred Learning ✦ Learning by Doing ✦ Learning with Values
OUR LEARNING PHILOSOPHY

Every Child Is a Unique Learner

We create an encouraging classroom environment where children can ask questions, explore new ideas, express themselves and discover their strengths at their own pace.

OUR APPROACH

How Learning Comes to Life

01

Experiential Learning

Children learn through hands-on activities, experiments, demonstrations and real-life experiences that help them understand concepts more clearly.

02

Activity-Based Learning

Games, puzzles, group tasks and interactive classroom activities encourage participation, logical thinking and joyful discovery.

03

Inquiry-Based Learning

We encourage children to ask questions, observe, investigate and explore ideas to develop curiosity, reasoning and problem-solving skills.

04

Creative Learning

Art, music, storytelling, role-play and imaginative activities help children express ideas and develop creativity, communication and confidence.

05

Collaborative Learning

Pair work, team projects and peer interaction nurture teamwork, empathy, respect, communication and the ability to learn together.

06

Individual Attention

Teachers observe each child's progress and provide suitable guidance, encouragement and learning support according to individual needs.
